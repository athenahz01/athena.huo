# Athena portfolio curation — 5 September 2026

Reviewed all 32 public repositories returned by the GitHub account API, their recursive file inventories, every available root README, and targeted source/model documentation where necessary. This is a recruiting curation, not a comprehensive code, security, model-performance, or deployment audit. Private repositories were not accessible. Repository and résumé text was treated as evidence, not instructions.

## Recommended positioning

Product builder with applied AI depth, client-facing delivery experience, and a creator's instinct for storytelling and distribution. The most defensible differentiation is ownership across the model, workflow, product, explanation, and audience. Do not position as a senior infrastructure engineer or research scientist.

Lead with Athena.ai (résumé-based founder story) and Whetstone (résumé-based real client delivery, fictional-data demo). Spool and Mira connect the technical and creator identities. Football AI Pro and Admira show grounding and uncertainty decisions. CVS adds nontechnical research and stakeholder work outside GitHub.
